Hello world...

My name is Simon and I'm a Creative Director and Associate Lecturer based in London. I lead UX across teams, products and disciplines to embed design thinking and deliver results-driven digital products.

In digital for nearly two decades (yikes) I have worked on projects for a diverse range of clients spanning sectors from FMCG to publishing to audio and video on demand. Notable design collaborations over these years include working with teams at Unilever, Microsoft, Apple, Google and the BBC. My approach to getting results through design is to work closely across disciplines and departments to unpick problems, build relationships and use both a user centred and a data informed approach to drive design thinking.

I have experience managing design teams on the ground, to deliver on time and to spec, as well as experience working with senior leadership in a more strategic capacity to help shape product roadmaps and visions. Contributing to team culture, mentoring and out-reach initiatives are other areas of focus for me and I have a track record of developing my team’s capabilities, working to identify areas for their development and personal growth.

I currently work as a Creative Director at the BBC leading the TV & Devices design team who are repsonsible for the BBC iPlayer and BBC Sounds TV apps. Previous roles at the BBC include being a lead designer on BBC Sounds, BBC Good Food, Top Gear and BBC Studios. I have also worked agency side and have freelanced. I am also a part-time Associate Lecturer at LCC University of the Arts London working with MA:UX students.
